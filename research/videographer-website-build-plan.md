# Harrison Wills Videographer Website Build Plan

Research source: `research/videographer-website-best-practices-2026.md`  
Use this file as the condensed implementation guide for future design and build work.

## Strategic Direction

Build a premium commercial creator website, not a generic videographer portfolio.

Harrison Wills should be positioned as a UK-based commercial videographer creating cinematic brand, fashion, lifestyle, hospitality, music, product, personal branding, and social-first content for clients across the UK and internationally.

Avoid wedding-first language and generic "I tell stories" positioning.

## Core Positioning Copy

Preferred H1:

Commercial videographer for fashion, lifestyle, hospitality and brand campaigns.

Supporting line:

Harrison Wills creates cinematic films, campaign assets and social-first video content for brands, artists, restaurants, models and creative teams across the UK and internationally.

Primary CTA:

Send a brief

Secondary CTAs:

- Watch reel
- View work
- Book a creative call
- Email Harrison
- DM on Instagram

## Homepage Sections

1. Hero
   - Full-bleed campaign still or short muted loop.
   - H1 and support copy above.
   - CTA buttons: Send a brief, Watch reel.

2. Featured reel
   - 45-75 second reel.
   - Short caption naming categories.
   - Use lightweight embed or self-hosted optimized video.

3. Selected work
   - 6-8 project cards.
   - Each card: still thumbnail, project name, category, role, one-line context.

4. Category pillars
   - Commercial video.
   - Fashion and beauty.
   - Lifestyle and hospitality.
   - Music artists.
   - Product content.
   - Social-first/personal branding content.

5. Proof strip
   - Client logos if available.
   - Otherwise use collaborators, restaurants, artists, models, venues, publications, and campaign facts.

6. Featured case study
   - Brief, approach, deliverables, result.
   - Include final film, stills, vertical cutdowns, and BTS where available.

7. Services snapshot
   - Link to service pages.
   - Frame services as outcomes and deliverables, not generic tasks.

8. Process
   - Brief/discovery.
   - Creative direction and shot plan.
   - Shoot/production.
   - Edit, colour, sound.
   - Platform cutdowns and delivery.

9. About Harrison
   - Concise personal paragraph.
   - Emphasise taste, commercial understanding, people/model direction, social-first delivery, UK/international availability.

10. Instagram/YouTube proof
   - Curated social proof only.
   - Do not embed a heavy raw Instagram feed.

11. Final CTA
   - "Have a campaign, launch, artist release or brand shoot in mind?"
   - CTA: Send a brief.

## Case Study Template

Use this structure:

1. Overview
2. The brief
3. Audience and platform
4. Creative direction
5. Production approach
6. Deliverables
7. Final film/assets
8. Results or client impact
9. Related work
10. Send a brief CTA

Prioritise case studies for:

- Fashion/beauty campaign.
- Restaurant or hospitality content system.
- Music artist release.
- Product launch.
- Personal brand/social content shoot.

## Contact and Lead Generation

Primary contact language:

Send a brief.

Contact form fields:

- Name.
- Email.
- Brand/company/artist name.
- Project type.
- Location.
- Timeline or shoot date.
- Budget range, optional.
- Project description.
- Links/references.

Form UX:

- Single column.
- Mobile-first.
- Direct email fallback.
- Do not force Calendly before qualification.

Calendly:

- Use as secondary CTA: Book a creative call.
- Best placed after form submission or on contact page.

Email capture:

- Do not launch a generic newsletter.
- Add later only if Harrison has a useful lead magnet and follow-up plan.

## Instagram Guidance

Use Instagram as:

- Discovery.
- Current proof.
- Behind-the-scenes validation.
- Collaborator and model credibility.
- DM channel.

Website implementation:

- Link in nav/footer.
- Add curated social highlights lower on homepage.
- Use selected Reels manually, not a heavy feed widget.
- Add UTM tracking to Instagram profile links.

## YouTube Guidance

Create `/youtube/` only when there is content ready.

Recommended content:

- Channel trailer/showreel.
- BTS videos.
- Director notes.
- Case-study breakdowns.
- Commercial videography advice for brands.
- Restaurant/hospitality content planning.
- Fashion/beauty shoot prep.
- Product launch video planning.

Implementation:

- Use lite embeds or thumbnail-triggered players.
- Add video summaries/transcripts where practical.
- Add VideoObject schema to video pages.

## SEO Requirements

Sitewide:

- Unique title tags.
- Unique meta descriptions.
- Clean URLs.
- Internal links between home, services, work, and case studies.
- Breadcrumbs on deeper pages.
- Optimized image alt text.
- Fast mobile performance.

Structured data:

- Person.
- Organization or LocalBusiness where appropriate.
- WebSite.
- BreadcrumbList.
- VideoObject on video/project pages.

Keyword themes:

- commercial videographer UK
- fashion videographer UK
- brand content creator UK
- hospitality video production
- restaurant videographer
- music video director
- product video creator
- social media content creator for brands
- personal branding videographer

Local SEO:

- Mention UK and international availability.
- Create location pages only with real project proof.
- Prioritise one strong UK page before city pages.

## Design Principles

The site should feel:

- Editorial.
- Cinematic.
- Premium.
- Modern.
- Confident.
- Social-native.
- Fast on mobile.

Avoid:

- Wedding tropes.
- Stock camera imagery.
- Heavy splash screens.
- Auto-playing audio.
- Huge raw Instagram feeds.
- Keyword-stuffed headings.
- Overlong generic biography copy.
- Showing too many projects at once.
- Pricing tables unless Harrison creates productised packages.

## Technical Notes

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript are sufficient for launch.

Use:

- Optimized poster images.
- Lazy-loaded video.
- Responsive images.
- Minimal JS.
- Accessible buttons and forms.
- Fast-loading thumbnails.

Avoid:

- Heavy animation libraries unless necessary.
- Full YouTube/Vimeo embeds in large grids.
- Third-party widgets above primary content.

## Measurement

Track:

- Send a brief clicks.
- Contact form submissions.
- Email clicks.
- Instagram outbound clicks.
- YouTube outbound clicks/plays.
- Portfolio filter use.
- Case-study visits.
- Service-page visits.
- Search Console queries.
- Mobile performance.

Recommended tools:

- Google Search Console.
- Lightweight privacy-conscious analytics or minimal GA4.
- UTM links from Instagram and YouTube.
