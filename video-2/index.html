The shift

One shoot a year
doesn't fill
a feed.

Brands are expected to show up every week now — on the grid, in stories, on the site, in paid social. A single campaign film cannot carry twelve months of that.

So the work is planned differently. One production day is built to produce a month of material: the hero film, the vertical cutdowns, the stills, the detail shots and the behind-the-scenes — shot together, graded together, delivered together.

It is a simpler, cheaper and far more consistent way for a brand to stay visible.

What Harrison shoots

Six ways
to work together

Photography sits inside every one of these. Stills and motion come off the same day, so the brand gets both without paying for two productions.

01

Content days

A full production day built around a month of output. One location, one crew, photo and video captured together and delivered as a ready-to-post batch.

02

Brand films

The hero piece for a launch, a new site or a campaign. Concept, shot plan, direction, grade and sound — with cutdowns built in from the start.

03

Social-first content

Vertical from the first frame. Reels, hooks and short edits composed for the format instead of cropped down from a wide cut afterwards.

04

Food & hospitality

Restaurants, bars, hotels and venues. Room atmosphere, service, plates and detail — shot quickly and quietly around a working floor.

05

Product & still life

Controlled, tactile product work: texture, packaging, use and scale. Motion for the ad, stills for the listing, both from the same set-up.

06

Photography

Portraits, campaign stills, interiors and lifestyle imagery — graded to match the video so everything looks like one body of work.

How it works

The monthly
set

The simplest way to keep a brand looking expensive all year: a repeating shoot day with a fixed, predictable delivery.

Step 01

Plan the month

A short call to agree what the brand needs to say next — the launches, the offers, the seasonal moments — and a one-page shot list built from it.

Step 02

Shoot the day

One day on location. Hero footage first, then the verticals, the stills, the details and the behind-the-scenes while the set is already lit and running.

Step 03

Deliver the batch

Everything graded, named, sized per channel and dropped into a shared folder within ten working days. No re-editing needed at the brand's end.

  • 1 hero film
  • 6–8 vertical cutdowns
  • 25+ edited stills
  • Detail & texture shots
  • Behind-the-scenes
  • Channel-ready exports

Studio

One person,

Harrison Wills is a photographer and videographer working with brands that need to look good constantly, not occasionally.

Shooting solo keeps it fast and unfussy: no production overhead, no six-person crew standing in a restaurant at service, no month-long approval chains. Just someone who plans the day properly, directs people gently and knows exactly what the edit needs before the camera goes up.
