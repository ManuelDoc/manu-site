The brief

What they needed

Salt Road had the opposite of a content problem on paper — a beautiful product and a beautiful coastline — and the opposite of one in practice: nobody had time to shoot any of it, so the feed went quiet for weeks at a time.

They wanted a rhythm they could rely on rather than another one-off campaign.

The approach

How it was shot

A standing monthly slot: one location day, planned against whatever the brand needs to say that month — a drop, a season, an event, a restock.

Because the day repeats, the planning gets cheap. A twenty-minute call, a one-page shot list, and the shoot is already built. Everything is graded to the same look so twelve months of content still reads as one brand.
