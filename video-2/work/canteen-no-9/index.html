The brief

What they needed

Canteen No.9 was opening on a busy street with three established competitors on the same block. The room was finished, the menu was set, and the launch was three weeks away — but there was nothing to put on a grid, in an ad, or on the website.

The brief was simple and blunt: give us enough material to look established from day one, and enough to keep posting for two months without booking another shoot.

The approach

How it was shot

Everything was shot in one day, split around service. Mornings for the room, the light and the empty-table detail; the pass and the plates during prep; the floor at full tilt through dinner.

Stills and motion were captured together rather than in separate passes, so the photography and the film share the same light, the same grade and the same evening.
