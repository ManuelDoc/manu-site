The brief

What they needed

A small-batch range launching direct to consumer, with paid social as the main channel. The existing pack shots were clean but lifeless, and the first ad tests were not holding attention past the second second.

The job was to make the products feel worth picking up: texture, weight, the sound of the lid, the way the light catches the finish.

The approach

How it was shot

A controlled table set-up with hard, directional light and a lot of macro. Each product got the same three moves — a reveal, a detail, a use — so the edits could be recombined into as many variants as the media buyer needed.

Stills were pulled from the same set-up, which meant the listing photography and the ad footage finally matched instead of looking like two different brands.
