04 — About

The person behind the lens.

I’m Harrison — a videographer from South Wales who grew up filming skate clips and never lost the pace. I like fast edits, bold sound and colour that makes a frame feel like the night actually felt.

I keep crews small and setups light, so the camera never kills the moment. You get one person to brief, quick answers and films delivered in days, not months.

  • Based in South Wales, filming UK-wide
  • Solo shooter or small crew
  • Edit, grade and sound in-house
  • Delivery in days, not months
